Useful to know

A few answers before you get in touch.

Some common questions about starting a conversation with Kaotech.

What types of projects does Kaotech work on?

Kaotech works across fuel stations, tank farms, water treatment systems and energy infrastructure. Specialist work includes site viability assessment, digital fuel management and operational monitoring.

Can Kaotech get involved before detailed design begins?

Yes. Early involvement can cover project scoping, site assessment, development readiness, feasibility considerations and the outline regulatory pathway before detailed design and major commitments.

Can projects involve both UK and African delivery?

Kaotech operates from London and Lagos and applies an international engineering perspective with local delivery knowledge. The right arrangement depends on project location, scope and required disciplines.

What should I include in my first enquiry?

Share the project type, location, current stage, expected timeline and the main support you need. It is fine to send an early outline if the scope is still developing.

Can I contact Kaotech directly by email?

Yes. Email info@kaotechdesigns.com or use the enquiry form above to prepare an email with your project details.
