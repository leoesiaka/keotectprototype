We support operators and developers from early site and facility definition through design development, technical review, procurement support and construction supervision.

  • Concept and detailed station design.
  • Site layout and facility planning.
  • Modular and skid-mounted station approaches.
  • Engineering drawings and calculations.
  • Applicable regulatory submissions and approvals coordination.
  • Equipment specification and technical procurement.
  • Construction supervision and quality checks.
  • Testing, commissioning and handover support.

Specialist solutions

Better decisions before capital. Better visibility after commissioning.

Two developing capabilities extend Kaotech's engineering offer into development assessment and connected fuel operations.

Development Readiness Package

Evidence before capital.

A proprietary, data-driven framework designed to evaluate whether a candidate site can be developed within an acceptable risk-return profile.

The approach brings together site, regulatory, competitive, commercial and operational considerations to inform an investment decision before major commitments are made.

  • Structured site and access review.
  • Regulatory and title considerations.
  • Competitive and demand context.
  • Weighted assessment and documented risks.
FuelSync platform

One operational picture for fuel assets.

FuelSync is Kaotech's SaaS platform concept for connecting pump and storage tank telemetry through web and mobile dashboards.

Its intended workflow connects, collects, stores, analyses and visualises operational data, supporting better awareness of asset activity and performance.

  • Connected pump and tank telemetry.
  • Centralised data collection and storage.
  • Web and mobile visualisation.
  • Platform development and pilot programme.
